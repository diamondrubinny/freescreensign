# Rubinny Trademark Policy (draft)

The code is free; the **name and logo** identify the official project so users
know what they are getting. This policy explains how you may use them.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product or service is "based on Rubinny", "powered by Rubinny"
  or "compatible with Rubinny".
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, tutorials and reviews.
- Distribute unmodified official releases under the Rubinny name.

## Please ask first

- Using "Rubinny" in the name of a product, company, domain or hosted service.
- Distributing a **modified** version under the name "Rubinny".
- Using the logo on merchandise.

## You may not

- Imply that your fork, service or company is the official Rubinny project or
  is endorsed by it.
- Use a confusingly similar name or logo.

Removing the "Powered by Rubinny" attribution required by LICENSING.md is not
allowed, even in renamed forks.

> Draft — trademark registration and this policy **require legal review**.
