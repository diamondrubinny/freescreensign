# Code of Conduct

Rubinny is a community project for everyone. We want participation to be
welcoming and harassment-free for all people, regardless of age, body,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.
- Help newcomers; every expert was once a beginner.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants who violate this code.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers via the contact listed in the
repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, chat, events)
and when someone officially represents the project in public.
